Trust & boundaries

Consent and research governance

A participant-owned record

  • Keep sources and approximate dates visible.
  • Separate consent for history, documents and re-contact.
  • Explain withdrawal and retention.

Historical family contributions

  • Review authority to contribute.
  • Protect relatives’ privacy and label recollections.
  • Establish ethics and governance.

A clear research boundary

  • Exclude raw AAC messages and personal voice recordings.
  • Require ethics and access review.
  • Avoid causal claims.

The demo’s limits

  • Browser-local prototype.
  • No clinical interpretation or real data release.
  • Recognition and tracking are simulations.
