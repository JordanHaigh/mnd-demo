# Suggested eight-minute conversation

1. Start on Demo overview. Explain the personal motivation and that both concepts are fictional prototypes. Open the presentation for the narrative, or begin Communicate’s guided demo.
2. In Quick Speak, choose Coffee. The visible message and browser speech demonstrate the one-tap interaction. Use Stop to demonstrate control.
3. In My boards, open Morning routine. Edit a button, save it and speak it. The editor supports labels, spoken text, original recordings, additions, removal and keyboard-accessible reorder controls.
4. In Type & speak, enter `can you`, choose a suggestion, edit and speak. In Handwriting, draw, try simulated recognition, review and speak. Describe recognition as a future integration.
5. In Voice Box, explain synthetic personal voice versus original audio. Before the meeting, record or import a short sample from someone who has agreed to demonstrate. Do not imply any sample is Margaret’s voice. No personal voice clone or ElevenLabs connection is active.
6. In Access & settings, choose Switch scanning. When focus is outside a form/button, Space or Enter activates the highlighted phrase. Directly focused buttons also work with keyboard selection. Try pointer dwell, labelled as a tracking simulation. The familiar phrases stay the same.
7. Show the passport and Hospital mode. Its fields are editable and printable. Then return to Overview and open MND Life.
8. In Life, filter timeline events, open a document’s provenance, add a potential life factor, and toggle local consent. Switch to the family and researcher perspectives. In the researcher perspective, open Case explorer. Choose NSW, then Hunter, and switch to Case connections to explore shared-factor links across regions. All 2,400 cases are fictional, with deliberately generated patterns. End with the co-design questions in slides 9 and 10.

Use the independent-prototype and synthetic-data labels throughout. The immediate invitation is feedback on AAC usefulness and gaps. The Life research concept needs separate expert work on ethics, governance, privacy and interoperability.

Before presenting: use a modern browser, enable audio, test microphone permissions on HTTPS/localhost, load the original audio sample you plan to use, and reset any unwanted local demo edits. The demo requires no AI, voice-provider or health-data API.
